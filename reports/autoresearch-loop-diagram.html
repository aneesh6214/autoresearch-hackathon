Self-Improving Tutor Optimization Loop

A research agent searches over tutor policies and response architectures. Candidate tutors are evaluated on benchmarked tutoring behavior, scored by an adversarial judge, and fed back into the next proposal round.

Feedback Signal

The winning strategy was not a generic "be helpful" tutor. The loop selected a two-step policy: diagnose_then_tutor. It first forms a hidden diagnosis, then writes a short student-facing hint that preserves agency and ends with one concrete next question.
